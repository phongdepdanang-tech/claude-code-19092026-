Our mission

Lower the barrier between a good product and a global market

Northwind started in 2016 as a two-person team frustrated by how hard it was to sell online outside your own country. Today we're 640 people across 11 offices, and the mission hasn't moved: give any seller — anywhere — the infrastructure that used to be reserved for companies a hundred times their size.

  • Sellers first. Every roadmap decision is weighed by what it unlocks for the merchant.
  • Borderless by default. Multi-currency, multi-language and local payment methods, not bolted on.
  • Boringly reliable. Payments settle on time, every time — that's the whole product.
